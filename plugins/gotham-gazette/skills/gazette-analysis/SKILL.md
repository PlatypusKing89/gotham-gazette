# Find the stories in verified numbers

Read [analytics](../gazette-weekly/references/analytics.md). Use decimal calculations or the bundled [calculator](scripts/calculate.py) with the packet described there. Never calculate decisive numbers by casual mental arithmetic. Preserve the raw packet and its source pointers.

Calculate all six margins, highest and lowest total, closest finish, largest blowout, all-play wins, and projection variance when original comparable projections exist. Verify totals and scope before claiming records. Compare against actual archived results, including prior weeks; 'closest this season through Week N' is different from 'closest ever'. Separate 2024-25 Classic scoring records from the 2026 Chaos era.

For a bench claim, check roster eligibility, actual starter replaced, bench points minus starter points, revised total, and opponent total. Describe the alternate result exactly. Do not call all bench points recoverable or combine impossible swaps. Identify hindsight as hindsight. Prove a decision was reasonably avoidable before calling it malpractice. A bye, IR, questionable, out, no-game player, empty slot, and active player with zero points are distinct states.

Read the previous published normal power board for competitive movement. An earlier draft of this week is never the baseline. If the latest published issue used a special board, show previous special placement separately and use the last normal board for normal movement, naming the baseline week. Do not subtract positions across different board types.

Research current injuries, IR placements, returns, role changes, and NFL news only where useful to Gotham. Use official team or NFL reports and other primary reporting, with publication/event dates and an as-of time. Do not recycle the PDF’s NFL status claims as current facts. Check contradictions against game results. Do not conflate an NFL season, fantasy season, or week. If the current facts cannot be verified, omit or explicitly qualify them. Do not make medical predictions.

Tie news and advice to roster impact under the verified Chaos rules, not a generic NFL recap. Waiver availability is only the supplied snapshot unless freshly checked. Do not report projection as expected usage, invent target shares, recommend rostered players as free agents, or assume current NFL team assignments. Verify Yahoo transaction and lineup-lock mechanics from current official Yahoo documentation before giving operational instructions. Cite external factual reporting in the issue’s human-readable sources area with working links.

Produce 3-5 story candidates with supporting facts, not just the top scoring team. Consider household matchups, divisional traffic, Monday-night swings, lineup holes, managers whose moves paid off, and real prior predictions. Let Pat’s narrative direction choose emphasis without overriding the facts. Assign every completed weekly issue a numeric editorial Chaos Index with visible component grades and limitations.
