# Approved Gotham Gazette standard

Use the final decisions in the source conversation, not its early brainstorm.

- Publication: Gotham Gazette. Tagline: The Newspaper of Record for Gotham City Fantasy Sports.
- Voice: inside the league, witty, direct, human, lightly rough around the edges. Approximate mix: 60% sports analysis, 30% smart comedy, 10% nonsense. Grade 8-9 readability. American English, no em dashes. Do not try to sound grand or literary. Avoid clichés and 'AI declarative' statements about what a result means.
- Headlines: team names. Stories: first names or approved public nicknames. Never public full first-and-last names, emails, or Yahoo profile links. Farrar and Henny are explicitly approved nicknames.
- Coverage: all six games and all 12 teams get substance every week. Neither the lead nor the art automatically features Pat. Known household relationships support jokes without explanatory introductions.
- Layout: approved Issue Zero v2 newspaper language, cream #f5f0e5, ink #171717, dark red #8a1f2d, strong serif masthead/headlines, clean body copy, columns, rules, score tables, captions, mobile-first reading. Reuse current source, do not rebuild from a generic template.
- Image pair: one hero plus one editorial cartoon, separate original assets. Strong focal point, simple scene, minimal text, league-specific subject. No newspaper mockup, combined composition, stick figures, automatic SVG substitutes, invented injury labels, or low-resolution stretching.
- Chaos: numeric 0-10 weekly index and a labeled five-level threat scale. Report component grades and editorial nature. Never assert an uncomputed standard-scoring flip.
- Boards: normal power rankings use the prior published normal board as the baseline. Special boards such as Chaos Agents or rooting interest must be labeled and kept distinct from standings and normal rankings.
- Editorial continuity: actual previous predictions, real earned corrections, ongoing supported stories, no recycled theme by default. Editor’s Note only when there is something to say.
- Publication: newest issue on homepage, permanent issue route, all old issues preserved in The Morgue archive. Keep publication metadata and social preview consistent.
- Timing: Tuesday 10 AM America/New_York is a target, not proof of an installed automation. Publish on demand when requested. Pat originally wanted review; later repeatedly authorized direct publication. Honor the current request and session authorization, without inventing a universal new approval requirement.
- Sending: website publication does not authorize emailing the league. Email and Yahoo API automation were aspirations, not verified installed systems.
